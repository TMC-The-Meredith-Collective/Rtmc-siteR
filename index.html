The problem we fix

You're running a serious business on systems that were never designed to scale with you. Your financials live across three spreadsheets that only one person understands. Your automations run until they don't — and nobody knows exactly when they stopped. Your workflows exist in someone's head, not in your infrastructure. When that person leaves, the business loses a system.

That's what we fix — permanently.

What we build

01 — Systems of Record

The numbers are true. In one place.

A single source of truth your whole operation runs on.

Consolidated books, automated reconciliation, and a data structure that returns the same answer whether you're talking to your bank, your auditor, or yourself.

02 — Systems of Action

Work gets done without you doing it.

Automation that runs even when you're not watching.
